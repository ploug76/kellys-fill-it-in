How to Play

Every word in the list fits in the grid exactly one way. There are no clues: the crossing letters must match.

Way 1: tap a word in the list, then tap its first square. The game figures out across or down.

Way 2: tap a square in the grid to see every word with that many letters, then pick the one you think goes there. Only 💡 Hint shows the answer.

Tap the same square again to switch between across and down. Placed words are crossed off the list.

💡 Hint fills in one word. ✔️ Check shows any word in the wrong spot. Solve with no hints for ★★★!
